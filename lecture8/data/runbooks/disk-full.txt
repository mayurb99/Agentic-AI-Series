Runbook: Disk Full (/var)

Symptoms:
- df -h shows /var above 85%
- Services fail to write logs
- Postgres or nginx may start throwing I/O errors

Checks:
1. df -h
2. du -sh /var/log/* | sort -h
3. Look for large rotated logs and leftover temp files

Fix:
1. Rotate and compress old logs.
2. Delete safe temp files under /var/tmp (only known junk).
3. Estimate days until threshold using current % and growth per day.
4. Do NOT delete database data directories.

Escalate:
If /var hits 95%, page platform on-call immediately.
